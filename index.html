Commercial plan: new-business deals from the commercial team, plus repeat loans from existing customers. Change new deals per year, loan size, pricing and repeat rate and the five-year view updates. Revenue is gross yield on the outstanding book, before credit losses and cost of capital.

Five-year view

Team rows stop at Year 2 because later headcount depends on automation still to be built. DTC and wholesale advances are repaid daily, so each one's balance runs down evenly to zero over its term. Revenue = outstanding balance × IRR ÷ 12 each month, so a 6-month €200k advance at 20% earns about €10k (5%). Year-end run-rate = December revenue × 12. Repeat loans are the same size, mix and price as new deals.
